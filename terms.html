What this is

A free tool from Koli Shema for building small daily habits in your marriage. That is all it is.

What this is not

This is not therapy, counselling, medical advice, or halachic guidance. It is a reminder app. If your marriage is in real difficulty, please speak to a qualified counsellor, a rav, or a trusted professional — an app cannot do that work.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is entered under your account. Do not use the app to store anything you would not want sitting on a server.

Availability

The app is provided as-is and free of charge. We try to keep it running and your data safe, but we cannot promise it will never be unavailable or that data will never be lost. Do not treat it as your only record of anything important.

Content

The habits, date ideas and prompts are suggestions, nothing more. Use judgement about what fits your marriage and your standards.

Ending use

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. We may suspend accounts that are being used abusively.
