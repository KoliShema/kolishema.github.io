Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Email

If you turn on emails in Settings, your address goes on a list Koli Shema can send to, together with the date you agreed. Turn it off and you come off that list. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. If you never turn it on, your address is only ever used to sign you in.

What we collect

Only two things: the email address you sign up with, and whatever you choose to enter in the app — which habits you marked done, your streak, your love notes, special dates, gift ideas and custom habits.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts or browsing activity. There is no analytics or tracking in this app, and no advertising.

Who can see it

Only you. Your data is locked to your account at the database level — no other user of this app can read it, and we have not built any feature that shows your entries to anyone else.

The administrators of this app can technically access the database, as is true of any app. We only do so to fix a problem, and we do not read your notes for any other reason.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, a hosting provider, on servers in the United States. Your password is encrypted and is never visible to us. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address — never your Google password.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting your account from the Settings screen removes your account and all your entries immediately and permanently. There is no recovery after that.

Your rights

You can download everything we hold about you at any time using "Download my data" in Settings, and you can delete all of it yourself from the same screen. You do not need to ask our permission or wait for us.

Children

This app is intended for married adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than quietly update this page.

Contact

Questions about your data: office.kolishema@gmail.com
